Brand Guidelines

A complete visual identity for HabitFlow — the logo system, color palette, typography, and usage rules. هوية بصرية متكاملة لـ HabitFlow: نظام الشعار، الألوان، الخطوط، وقواعد الاستخدام.

1. The Logo

HabitFlow is a flame that became a checkmark: the effort of building habits, resolving into achievement. The mark is a flame-teardrop carrying a check, set on a rounded tile with the indigo→violet gradient.

Primary mark

brand/logo-mark.svg — the primary mark. Always used on the gradient tile in full color.

Monochrome

logo-mark-mono.svg — ink flame, for print / single-color contexts.

On dark

logo-mark-white.svg — white flame + violet check for dark surfaces.

Horizontal / light

logo-horizontal.svg — for light backgrounds (UI, web).

Horizontal / dark

logo-horizontal-dark.svg — lighter gradient + light wordmark for dark surfaces.

Clear space

Keep a clear space equal to the height of the flame tip (⅓ of the tile) on all sides. Never place the logo inside a colored shape, add shadows, stretch, rotate, or recolor the gradient.

✓ Do

  • Use the full-color tile mark on light surfaces.
  • Use the white / mono variants when color is constrained.
  • Keep the flame tip pointing up.

✗ Don't

  • Don't change the gradient direction or colors.
  • Don't add outlines, drop shadows, or 3D effects.
  • Don't stretch, squish, rotate, or crop the flame.

The check inside the flame must stay legible at small sizes — minimum mark size is 16 px (UI) / 8 mm (print).

يجب أن تبقى علامة الصحّ داخل اللهب واضحة في الأحجام الصغيرة — أصغر حجم للشعار هو 16 بكسل في الواجهات و8 مم في الطباعة.

Contrast: white text on the gradient passes WCAG AA (≥ 4.5:1) against both indigo and violet. Never place primary text on the accent color alone.
التباين: النص الأبيض على التدرج يجتاز معيار WCAG AA (4.5:1) مع كلٍّ من النيلي والبنفسجي. لا تضع نصًا أساسيًا على لون التمييز وحده.

3. Typography

Display / headings Lexend — عادات تصنع الفرق
Body / UI Inter — Build habits that stick
Arabic Noto Sans Arabic — ابنِ عاداتك خطوة بخطوة

Stack: Lexend for display, Inter for UI/body, Noto Sans Arabic for Arabic (RTL). Fallback: Segoe UI / system-ui. Headings are font-display (Lexend), tight letter-spacing, semibold→bold. Body is Inter at 15–16px, line-height 1.6.

4. Assets & Regeneration

  • apps/web/public/brand/ — logo SVGs + og-image.png (1200×630).
  • apps/web/public/ — favicon.ico/svg, icon-192.png, icon-512.png, apple-icon.png (180), manifest.webmanifest.
  • Raster assets are generated by scripts/brand/render_logo.py (Python + Pillow). Re-run it after editing the geometry to keep PNGs in sync.

توليد الأصول النقطية يتم عبر سكربت Pillow — أعد تشغيله بعد أي تعديل على هندسة الشعار.

The flame geometry lives in two places only: the SVGs in public/brand/ and flame_polygon() in the render script. Keep them in sync.
