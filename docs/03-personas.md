# HabitFlow — Personas

## 1. The Ambitious Professional — "Maya"
- **Age/Gender:** 31 / Female
- **Role:** Product Manager at a SaaS company
- **Situation:** Juggling career growth, fitness, and side projects. Reads books on
  habit science. Uses Todoist + a paper journal today.
- **Goals:** Get promoted, run a half-marathon, write 20 min daily, reduce screen
  time. Wants structure without admin overhead.
- **Pain points:** Tools are siloed; no one connects sleep → focus → work output.
  Forgets to journal; streaks die after one bad day.
- **What HabitFlow must do:** Morning AI plan, burnout warnings before she
  overcommits, weekly review, auto time-blocking, cross-domain insights.
- **Tech profile:** iPhone + Mac, premium-plan comfortable, hates setup.

## 2. The Wellness Seeker — "Omar"
- **Age/Gender:** 24 / Male
- **Role:** Graduate student
- **Situation:** Recovering from a burnout episode. Meditates daily, tracks sleep,
  walks. Uses a wearable (Garmin).
- **Goals:** Stable sleep schedule, 8k steps, daily meditation, lower stress,
  balanced study load.
- **Pain points:** Anxiety spirals when streaks break; generic apps guilt-trip him;
  wearable data is in a separate app.
- **What HabitFlow must do:** Gentle, compassionate coaching; stress/mood trends;
  sleep integration; streak repair without shame; crisis-resource escalation.
- **Tech profile:** Android + Garmin, budget-conscious (Free/occasional Pro).

## 3. The Student — "Lina"
- **Age/Gender:** 19 / Female
- **Role:** University student (pre-med)
- **Situation:** Has exams, part-time job, and social life. Wants focus + grades.
  Uses Forest for focus and Notes for planning.
- **Goals:** Deep work 3×/week, Pomodoro routine, water intake, journaling for
  mental health.
- **Pain points:** Distractions; inconsistent planning; wants rewards and social
  accountability with friends.
- **What HabitFlow must do:** Pomodoro + focus mode, gamification (XP, badges),
  friend challenges, cheap/free tier, fun but not juvenile.
- **Tech profile:** Android + Windows laptop, low budget, loves badges/leaderboards.

## 4. The Busy Parent — "David"
- **Age/Gender:** 38 / Male
- **Role:** Sales director, two kids
- **Situation:** Little time, reactive schedule, wants health back but planning
  fails daily. Reads only during commute.
- **Goals:** Exercise 3×/week, read 10 min/day, limit after-dinner phone, family
  account with wife.
- **Pain points:** No time to log; overwhelmed by complex apps; needs one-tap
  logging and automatic plans.
- **What HabitFlow must do:** 30-second check-ins, quick add, AI that builds the
  day's plan for him, Family plan, forgiving streaks.
- **Tech profile:** iPhone, time-poor, willing to pay for convenience.

## 5. The Competitive Achiever — "Sara"
- **Age/Gender:** 27 / Female
- **Role:** Startup founder
- **Situation:** Loves numbers, streaks, self-quantification. Uses spreadsheets and
  smartwatch.
- **Goals:** Maximize productivity, hit aggressive goals, track everything with data.
- **Pain points:** Needs raw analytics, forecasts, heatmaps; hates vague "motivation."
- **What HabitFlow must do:** Deep analytics, scores, forecasts, AI explanations,
  export to CSV/PDF, Pro analytics.
- **Tech profile:** Latest iOS + Apple Watch, premium power user.

## 6. The Group Accountability Lead — "Coach Ray" (secondary)
- **Age/Gender:** 41 / Male
- **Role:** Fitness/life coach managing a small group
- **Situation:** Runs accountability circles; needs shared challenges and progress
  visibility for a small group.
- **Goals:** Run group challenges, see member participation, share templates.
- **Pain points:** Spreadsheets for group tracking; no lightweight tool.
- **What HabitFlow must do (v1.1+):** Group management, shared challenges,
  member activity feed, leaderboards. (Scoped as secondary; full B2B deferred.)
